Discussion
  • Shown so far, on archived recordings only: the results above.
  • Not shown yet: anything on the bench. Not claimed: any clinical rate or benefit to patients.
  • Limits: two arrays that sometimes disagree, one bench, one family of monitor.
  • Next: build the bench, pass five acceptance gates, record 337 sessions.
Conclusion

On archived recordings the monitor detects real faults but warns too late and too often to be relied on. The bench will show whether that belongs to brain recordings or to the method.
