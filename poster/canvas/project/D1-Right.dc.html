Acceptance gates: no result is read before these pass

On an apparatus that is too easy, a monitor has nothing to detect. Modulation depth is tuned until the decoder is as far above chance as the neural decoder was; if it cannot be, the phase stops.

12

Limits of inference, stated in advance

  • A camera in a sealed box is not an implanted array. A false-alarm rate measured here is not an estimate of the clinical rate.
  • Matching the decoding margin makes the problem comparably hard. It does not make the noise, the drift mechanism or the failure modes comparable.
  • One apparatus, one room, one family of monitor. P-7 narrows that objection and does not close it. The computational results rest on two arrays that disagree.
13

Conclusions

Computational: established

The monitor detects faults that counting activity cannot and does not mistake a task change for a fault. It is not deployable at the budget: 0 of 48 configurations pass, the median warning arrives 20 s (T11) and 35 s (T5) after the failure, and the risk score is never quiet within a session.
