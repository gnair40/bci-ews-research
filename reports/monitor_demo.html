What this demo is not hiding

One of the three episodes is caught. One is a real fault the monitor never reports. One is a healthy recording it alarms on anyway. That ratio is not a presentation choice — it is what the measurement found across 586 fault episodes and 265 healthy ones, at this exact threshold.

The monitor is not deployable at this operating point. What the measurements do support: it detects faults that leave overall activity unchanged, which counting spikes cannot see at all, and it separates a change of task from a change of hardware by a factor of 15 to 70.
