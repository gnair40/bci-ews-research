# Research Plan

## Working Title

Predictive Neural Stability: Engineering an Early-Warning System for Brain-Computer Interface Deterioration

## Working Research Question

Can early-warning signals detect impending deterioration in brain-computer interface neural signals before measurable performance degradation occurs?

## Status

Draft — research question and methodology are under investigation.

## Current Procedure

1. Conduct literature review.
2. Define BCI deterioration operationally.
3. Identify required data characteristics.
4. Evaluate candidate datasets.
5. Characterize selected data.
6. Develop preprocessing pipeline.
7. Establish baseline behavior.
8. Calculate candidate early-warning signals.
9. Align EWS with deterioration/performance measures.
10. Perform statistical validation.
11. Conduct surrogate/null testing.
12. Conduct robustness/sensitivity analysis.
13. Develop a predictive baseline if justified.
14. Validate on previously unseen data.
15. Interpret results and limitations.

## Important Constraint

The final dataset, preprocessing parameters, EWS features, and predictive model will be determined by the literature, data characteristics, and methodological validation rather than assumed in advance.
